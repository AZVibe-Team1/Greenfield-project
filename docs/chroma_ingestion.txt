Backend/Chroma Context (additional info):

There are 4 collections: Employer_Job_Descr_Collection, Employer_Skillswish_Collection, Seeker_Resume_Collection, and Seeker_Skills_Collection.
The Employer_JobDescr_collection will ingest all job descriptions for all employers. Each job description has a unique identifier (UUID) plus the text. There is also metadata that lists the job title, post date, and the unique employer identification number (also a UUID). So, this would allow a search to ‘find’ a job, then by looking at the metadata find the employer id, and from there do a mongodb search to pull up the rest of the employer information.
Employer_Skillswish_Collection is same basic principle, except this has the concatenation of an employer’s desired education level, education focus and desired skills. (NOTE, employers and seekers can define up to 15 skills, in free form text)
Seeker_Resume_Collection will ingest the resume. Its only metadata tag is the create_at datestamp. If we feel there should be more metadata here, its pretty straightforward to add more.
Seeker_Skills_Collection is the opposite of the Employer one
